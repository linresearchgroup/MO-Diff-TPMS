# Thirty five complete inverse-design comparisons

`components.csv` is final Supplementary Table S9: seven soft-material cases and 28 high-stiffness cases, 35 cases and 70 component results. No valid large or zero error is removed. `fixed_case_inputs_and_results.csv` supplies all six constituent moduli and saved t1/t2 for these same candidates. `summary.csv` is unchanged from the already computed 7/28-case statistics. Moduli and AE are in MPa; target AE = |Calculated - Target| and target APE = 100 * AE / |Target|. All paired targets are nonzero, and no denominator adjustment is used.

Soft-material target APE medians are 1.38%/4.64%, means 2.37%/4.63%, and maxima 8.01%/10.04% (storage/loss; seven cases each). The high-stiffness medians are 3.29%/3.32%, means 5.80%/5.73%, maxima 29.94%/26.85% (28 each). These selected cases are not a material-domain success-rate estimate. Seven zero or roundoff-level high-stiffness errors arise from equal assigned material fields, not distinct material structures.

The original S3-h record remains in `unpaired_components.csv` and `case_provenance.json`, outside the final table/statistics. Its exact target–candidate–calculation linkage is unresolved. Archived reference values are not substituted as confirmed calculations. S3-a/b/c/g have explicit correction records: fixed-candidate calculations replace the prior archived equalities with their target labels. Six soft-material checks use additional calculations, while S3-d uses its matched retained calculation. Source workbooks and per-case record locations are retained under `../source_records/` and in the provenance JSON.

Label-consistency errors in `../augmentation_label_check/` are a different task. Forward-surrogate errors compare prediction with MATLAB; screening errors compare prediction with target. They are not physical target errors. Completed diagnostic logs and residuals are under `../logs/`; no new calculations were run during this publication step.
